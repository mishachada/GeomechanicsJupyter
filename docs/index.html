Disturb the water.

Move through it, splash it, or let the rain do the work. This is an artistic physics-inspired model of motion-triggered dinoflagellate flashes.
Drag to trail light through the water · click/tap for a splash · hold for a stronger wake · press R to toggle rain · press C to clear.

The glow is deliberately short-lived: the interesting thing is not the organisms themselves, but the invisible motion field becoming visible.
