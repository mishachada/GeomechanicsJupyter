# Teaching Hub

Welcome to the Teaching Hub — an open collection of SAT Math and AP Physics resources designed for educators and self-guided learners. This site brings together study guides, practice problems, interactive demonstrations, data dashboards, and notebooks in one place.

## What's Here
- **SAT Guides**: Strategies and concept reviews crafted for the digital SAT.
- **Problem Bank**: Structured practice problems with metadata for easy filtering.
- **AP Physics 1 Units**: Lesson outlines and lab ideas aligned with the course framework.
- **Interactive Demos**: Browser-based explorations for math and physics concepts.
- **Dashboards**: Lightweight analytics powered by small CSV datasets.
- **Notebooks**: Executable explanations and visualizations rendered inline.

## Getting Started
1. Browse the [SAT section](sat/guides/reading-strategies/) for reading strategies.
2. Jump to [AP Physics 1 Kinematics](ap-physics-1/units/kinematics/) for a physics refresher.
3. Check back often as the problem bank, demos, and dashboards come online.

We update the site in phases and track progress in the project [CHANGELOG](../CHANGELOG.md).
