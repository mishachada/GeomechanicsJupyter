## Prompt
A circle has a radius of $6$ units. Central angle $AOB$ measures $75^\circ$. What is the length of arc $AB$ in units?

## Choices
A) $5\pi$
B) $\frac{5\pi}{2}$
C) $\frac{15\pi}{2}$
D) $\frac{5\pi}{3}$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>
Arc length equals $\ell = r \theta$ with $\theta$ in radians. Convert $75^\circ$ to radians: $\theta = 75^\circ \times \frac{\pi}{180^\circ} = \frac{5\pi}{12}$. Then $\ell = 6 \times \frac{5\pi}{12} = \frac{5\pi}{2}$. The correct choice is **B**.
</details>
