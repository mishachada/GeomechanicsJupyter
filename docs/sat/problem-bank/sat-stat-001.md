## Prompt
Five students record the number of practice questions they solve in a week: $12, 15, 18, 20,$ and $x$. The mean number of questions is $17$. What is the value of $x$?

## Choices
A) $17$
B) $18$
C) $19$
D) $20$

<details><summary>Solution</summary>
Let $S$ be the sum of the five values. The mean condition gives $\frac{S}{5} = 17$, so $S = 85$. The known values add to $12 + 15 + 18 + 20 = 65$. Therefore $x = 85 - 65 = 20$. The correct choice is **D**.
</details>
